# Marketing

Produce useful marketing work from the context available in the conversation and repository. Do not depend on a private brief or an external framework library.

## Start with the decision

Identify the business goal, audience, offer, desired action, channel, constraints, and available evidence. Ask only for missing information that would materially change the answer; otherwise state reasonable assumptions and proceed.

Never fabricate metrics, customer quotes, research, testimonials, or product capabilities. Do not include personal data in examples. Treat outbound messages and campaign assets as drafts unless the user explicitly authorizes sending or publishing them.

## Choose the right approach

| Task                    | Focus                                                                                                            |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Positioning or strategy | Audience problem, alternatives, differentiated value, proof, and one measurable objective                        |
| Copy or landing page    | Message hierarchy, specificity, objections, proof, friction, and one clear call to action                        |
| CRO or funnel review    | Locate the largest uncertainty or drop-off, form a testable hypothesis, and define success and guardrail metrics |
| SEO or content          | Search intent, information gaps, credibility, internal links, and a distribution plan; avoid keyword stuffing    |
| Pricing or packaging    | Customer value, segments, willingness-to-pay evidence, package boundaries, and upgrade logic                     |
| Launch or campaign      | Audience, promise, proof, channel sequence, owner, timing, budget, and measurement                               |
| Email, ads, or social   | One audience and one job per asset; earn attention quickly and match the destination page                        |
| Onboarding or retention | Time-to-value, activation event, friction, expectation setting, and reasons users disengage                      |

## Deliver

Lead with the recommendation or finished draft. Separate observed facts from assumptions, explain the few choices that matter, and include concrete next steps. For audits, prioritize findings by likely impact and confidence. For experiments, specify the hypothesis, variant, primary metric, guardrails, duration or sample-size decision, and stopping rule.

Match the product's established voice when examples are available. Otherwise use clear, specific language and label the proposed voice so the user can redirect it.
